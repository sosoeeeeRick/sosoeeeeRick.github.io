I am currently a master's student at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) (SJTU), advised by Prof. [Hesheng Wang](https://irmv.sjtu.edu.cn/wanghesheng). I received my bachelor's degree from SJTU, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. [Lijun Han](https://irmv.sjtu.edu.cn/lijun) and Prof. [Fan Xu](https://irmv.sjtu.edu.cn/xufan). Since the summer of 2026, I have also been fortunate to collaborate with Prof. [Zackory Erickson](zackory.com) at Carnegie Mellon University.

My research focuses on developing adaptive robot policies for physical human–robot interaction (pHRI), with particular interests in personalized physical assistance and shared control.
